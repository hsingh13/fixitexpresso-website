What TurboChef models do you repair? +

We repair all TurboChef speed oven models including NGC, NGO, Sota, Encore, Fire, Bullet, i3, i5, Plexor, Tornado, ECO, C3, C4, and HhC. Our factory-trained technicians have 21+ years experience with both current and legacy TurboChef models.

What equipment brands do you service? +

We specialize in TurboChef speed ovens, the most common models in the Seattle area. We also repair MerryChef speed ovens (eikon and conneX series) and other commercial kitchen equipment. Call us to discuss your specific needs.

Do you also repair MerryChef ovens? +

Yes. While TurboChef is our primary specialty, we also service MerryChef speed ovens in the Seattle area, including the eikon series (e1s, e2s, e3, e4, e6) and conneX series (11, 12, 16). Our technicians diagnose and repair MerryChef heating issues, magnetron failures, control board problems, door faults, and error codes. See our MerryChef common problems guide for E101–E117 error code help, or call 425-547-0200 to discuss your repair.
